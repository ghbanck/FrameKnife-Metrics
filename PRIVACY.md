# FrameKnife Metrics Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FrameKnife Metrics is a screen ruler, grid and guide overlay for Windows. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.

## What the app keeps

The app saves your layouts and settings on your own computer, in `%USERPROFILE%\.frameknife`. They never leave your computer unless you export a layout to a file yourself. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes everything it saved.

## What the app does not do

- It makes no network connections, so nothing you measure, draw or type is sent anywhere.
- It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- It does not read your files, other than the layout files you choose to import.
- It does not capture or record your screen. The overlay only draws on top of it.

## Microsoft Store

When you get the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, licensing and updates under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

## Contact
